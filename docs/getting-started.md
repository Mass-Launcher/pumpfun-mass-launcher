# Getting started with the pump.fun mass launcher

Step-by-step guide to your first mass launch on pump.fun via MassLauncher.

## Step 1: Sign up

Go to **[masslauncher.fun](https://masslauncher.fun)** and click **Get started**.

Pick a sign-in method:

- **Telegram** — link via the bot's `/weblogin` code (no email needed)
- **Google** — one-click OAuth
- **Email + password** — 6-digit code verification

A Solana deposit wallet is created for your account automatically. You don't need to install Phantom or any other browser wallet.

## Step 2: Deposit SOL

Your dashboard shows a personal Solana deposit address (and a QR code). Send SOL to it from:

- Any Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack)
- An exchange (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken — withdraw to your deposit address)

Credits land in your account within **~30 seconds** of the deposit confirming on-chain.

**Minimum recommended deposit**: 0.5 SOL (enough for 25 launches with room to spare).

## Step 3: Configure a coin

Go to **Coins** and create a profile. You can save up to 3:

- **Name** — e.g. "Mass Launch SPAM"
- **Ticker** — e.g. "SPAM"
- **Description** — optional, shows on pump.fun
- **Image** — required, will be uploaded to pump.fun's IPFS
- **Socials** — Twitter, Telegram, website (all optional)

You can re-use a single profile across many mass launches, or rotate between profiles.

## Step 4: Fire your first mass launch

Go to **Launch**, pick:

1. A coin profile
2. A quantity (1, 5, 10, 25, etc. — up to your daily cap)

Click **Launch**.

Within ~10-15 seconds you'll see pump.fun links for each minted coin. They're all live on the new-pairs feed.

## Step 5: Watch the feed

Open pump.fun's new-pairs feed in another tab. Your coins will appear within a single Solana slot of each other.

## Tips for effective mass launches

- **Vary the images** — launching 25 identical-looking coins is less effective than 25 with different art. Use a folder of images and the bot will rotate through them.
- **Reference your main token** — put your main coin's CA in the description so attention flows back.
- **Time it with momentum** — mass-launching during a hot trending coin gets more eyes.
- **Use distinct names** — small variations on the ticker increase coverage of pump.fun search.

## Common issues

### "Insufficient credits"
Top up your deposit wallet. Credits land within 30s of confirmation.

### "Daily cap reached"
The 200-launch soft cap protects the wallet pool. Contact support for a higher cap.

### "Launch failed"
Failed launches are automatically refunded as credits. Check the pump.fun link in your History — usually it's a transient RPC issue and you can re-fire.
